## 👨🏻‍💻 About Me:

## 📖 Classes and Extracurriculars:
📝 I am currently enrolled in __COMP 311__ (Computer Organization), __COMP475__ (2D Graphics), __MATH347__ (Linear Algebra), and __BUSI408__ (Corporate Finance). 

🗒️ Previous courses I have taken include COMP 210 (Data Structures and Analysis), COMP 211 (Systems Fundamentals), COMP 290 (Essential Tools for Computer Science), COMP 301 (Foundations of Programming), COMP 455 (Models of Languages and Computation), COMP 550 (Algorithms and Analysis), and COMP 110 (Introduction to Programming and Data Science). In COMP 210, I was introduced to the time complexities of searches, insertions, and deletions in the most frequently used data structures, and learned to recognize the optimal data structure to use in a given scenario. In COMP 211, I drew connections between high-level programming languages and lower levels of abstraction. In COMP 290, I learned the principles of behind the command line interface, Docker, and Git. In COMP 301, I expanded my understanding of OOP and learned industry-standard design patterns like MVC. In COMP 455, I discovered the limitations of algorithms and the idea of "hard" computational problems. In COMP 550, I practiced problem-solving methods like greedy processing and dynamic programming for complex problems that can be divided into subproblems. This all started in COMP 110, where I learned foundational programming concepts such as data types, boolean logic, writing functions, control flow, and recursion. I'm now an Undergraduate Teaching Assistant for this course and love assisting students who are beginning their programming journey!

📱 I'm pursuing iOS development as a member on one of [App Team Carolina's](https://appteamcarolina.com/) Production teams. Having progressed through ATC's learning teams diving into industry standard iOS development practices, I'm now working as an iOS Developer for Luminary: an app focused on advancing accessible navigation at UNC by providing real-time updates on campus obstructions and adaptive navigation options. I also serve as the club's Chief Learning Officer: I oversee and support ATC's iOS and UI/UX learning teams which reaches 100+ students each semester.
